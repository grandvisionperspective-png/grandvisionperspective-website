The type stack

Fraunces and Manrope.

Fraunces (variable serif, optical sizing) for display and headlines. Manrope (geometric sans, weight 300 to 700) for body and UI. Italic Fraunces in Gold is the brand signature; use for emphasis within serif headlines.

Display + Headlines
A grand vision, executed precisely.
Fraunces is a variable serif with proper optical sizing. The italic carries the brand's gold-accent signature. Use weights 400 to 700 across the type scale.
font-family: 'Fraunces', Georgia, serif;
font-variation-settings: 'opsz' 144;
Body + UI
The dashboard layer your team actually uses.
Manrope is a modern geometric sans with weight axis 300 to 800. Use for body copy, captions, labels, and UI. Pairs warm with Fraunces; reads modern without being eccentric.
font-family: 'Manrope', sans-serif;
weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700

Brand story · 120 words · primary

Grand Vision Perspective was started in Bali by Barrie, after two decades watching the same pattern play out across franchises, startups, hospitality groups, and growing companies on three continents. Businesses that began with real purpose would slowly lose touch with the people they were built to serve. Margins tightened, processes bloated, quality slipped, and the team carrying it all began to break. The root cause was almost always the same: broken systems putting unnecessary pressure on good people. GVP exists to fix that. We design and ship the operating systems that let businesses scale without grinding down the humans inside them.

Positioning · 1 paragraph · external

Privacy rules

How we represent ourselves, and why.

A short list of the rules that govern how Grand Vision Perspective shows up in the world. These are not preferences. They are commitments to the clients who trust us with their operations.

  • 01 First name only. The founder is referenced as "Barrie" externally. Surnames are never used on the public site, in press, in partner briefs, or in any media represented to the outside world.
  • 02 No physical address. Geographic positioning is "Based in Bali. Clients across Europe, the US, and Asia." Indonesia-registered legal entity. No specific city beyond the regional anchor of Bali, no street address, no co-working location named.
  • 03 Projects are anonymised. We do not trade on our clients' names. Industry and shape of the engagement are described; specific clients are never identified. No client logos. No recognisable brand names.
  • 04 Contact via form or email only. No phone number on the site. No address. Contact happens through the form on this site or via connect@grandvisionperspective.com.
  • 05 No AI-generated faces, ever. The brand promise depends on trust; synthetic people erode that. All photography is real, and where people appear, they are named with consent.
