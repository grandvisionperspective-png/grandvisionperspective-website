Who we work with

Founder-led businesses that have outgrown the tools and habits they started with. Our client work spans hospitality, food and beverage, and services businesses.

  • Founder-led companies where the founder still touches operations.
  • Operations-heavy businesses where at least one process has drifted and now costs real time.
  • Multi-entity groups whose finance lead has stopped trusting the numbers.
  • Teams that want a way of working they own, not a tool they rent.

02

What we do

We work on how the business runs, not only the tools it runs on. Automation is one method among several, used where it removes repetitive work.

Management and Operations Consulting

Operational audits, process mapping, and a clear redesign of how work moves through the business: sales pipeline, approvals, back office and management reporting.

Strategic Advisory

Roadmaps, priorities and hands-on guidance for founders and operators. We find where friction is costing time and money, and show how to remove it.

Implementation Oversight

A phased implementation plan, coordination with your team and chosen vendors, and automation only where it genuinely removes repetitive steps. Training and documentation included.
