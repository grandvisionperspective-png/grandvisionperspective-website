# GVP brand rules (internal)

Moved here from the public /media/ page in October 2026, when that page became the Company Profile. These are for the team and partners producing GVP material. They are not published on the site.

## Representation and privacy

1. **First name only.** The founder is "Barrie" externally. No surname on the public site, in press, partner briefs or any outward-facing material.
2. **No address.** Public positioning is "Based in Bali. Clients across Europe, the US, and Asia." No city beyond Bali, no street address, no co-working space. The registered street address never appears on the site, including /terms/. Terms identify the seller as registered in Gianyar, Bali; full details go on invoices and are sent on request.
3. **Projects are anonymised.** Describe the industry and the shape of the work. Never client names, logos or recognisable brand names. Founding-client case studies are anonymised too.
4. **Contact by form or email only.** No phone number. Contact form or connect@grandvisionperspective.com.
5. **No AI-generated faces.** Where people appear, they are real and named with consent. No photos of Barrie.

## Positioning (KBLI 70209)

- Management and operations consultancy: strategic advisory, operations audits, implementation oversight.
- Automation is a method, never the product. No software-build, IT, bookkeeping or tax implications. Bookkeeping and tax stay with each client's accountant.
- No standalone digital-product SKUs on the GVP site. ClearlyPlannedStudio stays fully separate, with no links either way.
- Tagline (approved, Oct 2026): "We design operations that let people be people." Retired: "We build systems that let people be people."

## Logo

- Vantage mark: one observer point above four widening horizon bars.
- Lockups: horizontal (default), vertical, mark only, wordmark only. Each in coloured (gold), black and white. Files in brand/assets/ and as a zip at brand/assets/gvp-logo-files.zip.
- Use the variant with the strongest contrast against the background. Do not recolour, stretch or redraw.

## Type

- Fraunces (variable serif, optical sizing) for display and headlines.
- Manrope (geometric sans, weights 300 to 700) for body and UI.
- Italic Fraunces in gold: once per page, in the main headline only. Elsewhere italics inherit the text colour.
- No eyebrow-with-line labels, no gold underline rules, no rows of three gold-bordered cards.

## Footer mark

"A GRAND VISION, EXECUTED PRECISELY . 2023" stays in the site footer. Not used in press material.

## Copy

British English. No em or en dashes. No invented facts, numbers, clients or quotes. Testimonials are quoted as published.

## Prices: IDR only

Bank Indonesia bans dual quotation (PBI 17/3/2015 art. 11, SEBI 17/11/DKSP part II). Every public page, file and structured-data block shows prices in Indonesian rupiah only, formatted Rp45.000.000. No prices in any other currency, no currency pairs, no currency toggles or converters, and no approximate foreign-currency notes. Clients outside Indonesia may ask for a quote in their own currency, confirmed on the invoice, never on the site.
