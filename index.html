Client result

A hospitality group with too much finance admin done by hand.

90%

less manual finance admin, through process redesign

The problem

Several entities, and a lot of manual finance admin between them: transactions, reconciliation handoffs, and management reports.

What we changed

We redesigned how transactions, reconciliation handoffs, and management reports flowed across the entities, then oversaw the implementation.

The result

Manual finance admin fell by 90%. Bookkeeping and tax stayed with the group's accountant.
