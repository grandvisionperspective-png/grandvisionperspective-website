Intake

Before we draft your proposal.

A short structured set of questions so we can write a proposal that actually fits your situation, not a template. About fifteen minutes to fill in. Everything you tell us is covered by the mutual NDA we discussed on the strategy call.

If a question does not apply, leave it blank. If a question needs a long answer, type it long; we read everything carefully before we propose.

Section 1

About you.

Who you are and what your business does, briefly.

Section 2

Team and stage.

Where the business is in its life right now.

Section 3

The operational reality.

The honest version of what is actually happening today.

Section 4

What you have tried.

Helps us avoid repeating moves that did not work.

Section 5

What good looks like.

Three short questions on outcome. Vital for shaping the proposal.

Section 6

Practical details.

Timing, money, decision process.

Section 7

Anything else.

Free space.

We read everything carefully and reply within two working days with the proposal or a note on why we are not the right fit.
